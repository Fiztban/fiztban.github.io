Psychiatric Assessment

A one-session comprehensive psychiatric assessment to investigate and diagnose potential mental disorders or injuries of children and young people. A report will be written and available two weeks after the appointment.

Assessment duration: 1½ hours.

Cost: $650 inc GST

Therapeutic Interventions

We can offer a range of therapeutic options including talking therapies and onward referral to other professionals if required. These include:

  • Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing Therapy (EMDR)
  • Mentalisation Based Therapy (MBT) for Adolescents
  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (Including Trauma Focused CBT)
  • Dialectical Behavioural Therapy (DBT)
  • Family Based Treatment for Anorexia Nervosa. (FBT-AN)

Assessment duration: tailored and can be discussed.

Cost: dependent on intervention.

Psychiatric Medication Consultation

Medication can be considered and prescribed if appropriate (Including special authority medications). Ongoing follow up and review can be provided.

Assessment duration: 1 hour.

Cost: $500 inc GST
