    To be able to start booking the next steps, we need both of the following completed:

    • Patient Registration Form for your child
    • Consent and Terms of Service Form(s)

    Which Consent and Terms of Service Form(s) are needed depends on whether your child can give consent for themselves.

    If the young person is 16 or older

    Under the Care of Children Act 2004 they consent for themselves, so only one Consent and Terms of Service Form is needed, completed by them. The Patient Registration Form can be completed by the young person or by any of their parents or guardians.

    If the child is under 16

    We require consent from all legal guardians for our non-routine psychiatric services, so two copies of the Consent and Terms of Service Form are needed — one for each parent or legal guardian.

    We will email you a personalised link with your own forms ready to complete.

    Is your child 16 or older?

    Because your child is 16 or older, they consent for themselves under the Care of Children Act 2004. So we only need one Consent and Terms of Service Form, completed by your child.

    The Patient Registration Form can be completed by either your child or any of their parents or guardians.

    Parents and guardians are still welcome to be involved throughout the assessment — this is separate from the consent process.

    If your child may need support to make this decision, please contact us before completing the forms.

    • Consent and Terms of Service Form Completed by your child Open form

    Please note that under the Care of Children Act 2004, the other parent or legal guardian still needs to be informed and to provide consent — even if you are separated, or if they currently reside outside New Zealand.
