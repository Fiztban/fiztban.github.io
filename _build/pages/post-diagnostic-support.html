This guide is designed to cover everything you need to know about post-diagnostic support and what it includes.

A diagnosis is a beginning, not an end. Post-diagnostic support gives you time with one of our clinicians to make sense of the diagnosis, work through the things you are finding hard, and build practical strategies — so you leave feeling informed, supported and ready for the next step.

Who this is for

Post-diagnostic support is for anyone with an ADHD or autism diagnosis, and for their family — whether that diagnosis came from us or from somewhere else. There are three ways people reach it.

  • After an assessment with usSupport follows on directly from the diagnosis appointment.
  • Diagnosed elsewhereWe accept referrals from a GP or another referrer, and directly from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Where no diagnosis was givenA session is still worth having, and we can look at what else may be going on.
For adults

If you are an adult seeking support for yourself, the pathway is the same — please contact us and we will talk it through with you.

Appointment options

Post-diagnostic support sessions can be held either in person at our Whangārei clinic, or online via telehealth. A sensory assessment with our Occupational Therapist is carried out in person.

What we can explore together

There is no set script — we follow what matters most to you. A session usually covers some of the following.

  • Understanding the diagnosisWhat ADHD or autism means for your child and your family.
  • Exploring specific difficultiesTalking through the particular challenges you are experiencing.
  • Non-medication strategiesPractical tools and approaches for everyday life, at home and at school.
  • Planning the next stepsWhere to go next, including a pathway into our ADHD Titration Clinic if medication is being considered.

Even without a diagnosis, we are here to help. If an assessment does not lead to a diagnosis, a post-diagnostic support session is still valuable — we can look at other strategies that may help you and your family.

  2. 15 minutes By phone Nurse Specialist Free

    Support begins with a free 15-minute phone call with one of our Nurse Specialists. It is a chance to tell us what you are hoping to get from a session, so we can make sure the time is spent on what matters most to you.

    Because post-diagnostic support is being arranged on its own rather than following an assessment with us, this call is also how we make sure it is the right fit before anything is booked or charged.

    Just let us know a good time and we will arrange it.

  3. After the call we will help you book your session on our Client Booking Portal, in person at our Whangārei clinic or online, whichever suits you.

    Please book the included session within a month of the diagnosis.

  4. 1 hour In person or online Nurse Specialist

    A relaxed, one-to-one hour. There is no set script — we follow what matters most to you, working through the diagnosis, the challenges you are facing, and the strategies most likely to help.

    If medication is being considered, this session can open the door to our ADHD Titration Clinic. If you would rather focus on non-medication approaches, we will talk through the strategies that fit.

  5. Additional post-diagnostic support sessions are available any time they would be helpful, at $150 per hour (inc GST). Some families come back once; others check in as things change.

    Our programmes run alongside this support. Just get in touch when you would like to arrange something.
