What “titration” means

Titration is the process of starting an ADHD medication and carefully adjusting the dose — a little at a time — until we find the amount that works well and is safe. While we do this, our Clinical Nurse Specialist keeps a close eye on how things are going, week by week.

What’s included every month

  • Weekly nurse follow-up
  • Review of your weekly observations
  • Advice on any dose changes
  • Your prescriptions for the month, organised by the nurse

Dose adjustments and medication switches

Dose adjustments are handled by our nurse as part of your monthly follow-up. A change to a different medication is at Dr Castle’s discretion — it may involve an additional 15-minute doctor’s appointment ($100 inc GST) to discuss the switch, or may be managed by our Clinical Nurse Specialist.

Your prescriptions

    Because we made the diagnosis, this first appointment is $250 (inc GST).

  2. Every week, while titrating

    Each week we need four simple physical observations. These help us keep you safe and spot any changes early.

    • Height
    • Weight
    • Blood pressure
    • Pulse

    Where to get these done

    You can have them measured at your GP or at a local pharmacy. The most important thing is to use the same equipment each time — that way we can see genuine trends over the weeks, rather than differences caused by different machines.

    Please email your results to info@kinderminds.nz each week, and we will let you know if anything needs to change.

  3. Clinical Nurse Specialist Weekly

    Our Clinical Nurse Specialist reviews the observations you send in (step 3) and follows up with you each week, adjusting the dose a little at a time until we find the amount that works well and is safe.

    Dose adjustments are handled by the nurse as part of this follow-up. A change to a different medication is at Dr Castle’s discretion, and may involve an additional 15-minute doctor’s appointment.

  4. Once the dose works well and is safe, things settle. Weekly checks are no longer needed as often, and we start preparing to hand ongoing prescribing back to your GP.

  5. Once you are settled on a stable, safe dose, we write a discharge letter to your GP asking them to take over ongoing prescribing. This is included at no extra cost.

    We first confirm your GP is happy to take over. Once care is handed back, your GP looks after all of your ADHD care from that point.

Other situations

Just need a Special Authority?

A Special Authority is an approval that lets your GP prescribe ADHD medication with the government subsidy. This is a commencement only, with no ongoing monitoring by us: we confirm it is safe to start, discuss the choice of medication, and write to your GP to set up the Special Authority — under the $250 first-appointment fee. We would first need email confirmation that your GP is happy to manage the titration.

If your GP cannot take over

We can keep monitoring and prescribing for you at $150 per calendar month, or $50 per calendar month for atomoxetine only.

Coming back later

If you have been discharged and later need a dose change or medication review, whether a doctor’s appointment is needed — or whether it is a simple dose change our Clinical Nurse Specialist can manage — is at the doctor’s discretion. A new calendar-month monitoring period begins so we can follow you up safely.
