Our goal is simple: to find the right medication and dose safely and with close support, and then hand care back to your GP once things are settled.

What “titration” means

Titration is the process of starting an ADHD medication and carefully adjusting the dose, a little at a time, until we find the amount that works well and is safe. While we do this, our Nurse Specialist keeps a close eye on how things are going, week by week.

The two parts of the clinic

Our ADHD Titration Clinic has two separate components, explained below:

  • Medication ConsultationA 30-minute appointment to commence or review your ADHD medication. We cover medication safety, talk through the options based on your child’s needs, and agree on a plan. Medication can be started immediately after the appointment. We charge a single fee for this appointment.
  • Titration & MonitoringThe medication titration can take between 1 and 2 months, where we adjust the dose and monitor the effects. We follow your child week-to-week with check-ins and physical observations. This is supervised by Dr Castle and led by our Nurse Specialists. It is charged as a monthly subscription, which includes all your prescriptions.

You are welcome to have the Medication Consultation with us and the titration elsewhere. Where the titration happens is your choice, and it is covered under Titration & Monitoring.

Who this clinic is for

The ADHD Titration Clinic is for anyone with an ADHD diagnosis who is starting medication, or who is already on it and needs it reviewed. The diagnosis can be ours or from somewhere else.

  • Diagnosed by Kinder MindsMedication follows on from the assessment. We already hold everything we need.
  • Diagnosed somewhere elseWe review that assessment first, to confirm the diagnosis and that it is safe to begin.

Dose adjustments and medication switches

Dose adjustments are handled by our nurse as part of your monthly follow-up. A change to a different medication is at Dr Castle’s discretion — it may involve an additional 15-minute doctor’s appointment ($100 inc GST) to discuss the switch, or may be managed by our Nurse Specialist.

  6. Every week, while titrating

    Each week we need four simple physical observations. These help us keep you safe and spot any changes early.

    • Height
    • Weight
    • Blood pressure
    • Pulse

    Where to get these done

    You can have them measured at your GP or at a local pharmacy. The most important thing is to use the same equipment each time — that way we can see genuine trends over the weeks, rather than differences caused by different machines.

    Please email your results to info@kinderminds.nz each week, and we will let you know if anything needs to change.

  7. Nurse Specialist Weekly

    Our Nurse Specialist reviews the observations you send in (step 4) and follows up with you each week, adjusting the dose a little at a time until we find the amount that works well and is safe.

    Dose adjustments are handled by the nurse as part of this follow-up. A change to a different medication is at Dr Castle’s discretion, and may involve an additional 15-minute doctor’s appointment.

  8. Once the dose works well and is safe, things settle. Weekly checks are no longer needed as often, and we start preparing to hand ongoing prescribing back to your GP.

  9. Once you are settled on a stable, safe dose, we write a discharge letter to your GP asking them to take over ongoing prescribing. This is included at no extra cost.

    We first confirm your GP is happy to take over. Once care is handed back, your GP looks after all of your ADHD care from that point.

Other situations

Just need a Special Authority?

A Special Authority is an approval that lets your GP prescribe ADHD medication with the government subsidy. This is a commencement only, with no ongoing monitoring by us: we confirm it is safe to start, discuss the choice of medication, and write to your GP to set up the Special Authority — under the $250 Medication Consultation fee. We would first need email confirmation that your GP is happy to manage the titration.

If your GP cannot take over

We can keep on with Titration & Monitoring for you at $150 per calendar month, or $50 per calendar month for atomoxetine only.

Coming back later

If you have been discharged and later need a dose change or medication review, whether a doctor’s appointment is needed — or whether it is a simple dose change our Nurse Specialist can manage — is at the doctor’s discretion. A new calendar-month Titration & Monitoring period begins so we can follow you up safely.
