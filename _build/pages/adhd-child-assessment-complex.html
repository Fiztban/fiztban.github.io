About ADHD assessment diagnostic criteria

An ADHD assessment relies on observations made in separate environments in order to meet diagnostic criteria. This means that ahead of the appointment that decides the diagnosis, we need to collect information in the form of a parental interview and specialised questionnaires. Without these we will not be able to complete the assessment.

Two questionnaires are sent to a teacher who knows your child well. We recommend a teacher who has known your child for at least six months. If we are undertaking the assessment at the start of an academic year, or after a recent school change, then your child’s previous teacher is usually the best candidate for this part of the assessment.

  1. We have received your child’s referral, from either yourself or your GP or referring person. On review of the information provided to us, we would be delighted to see your child for an ADHD Assessment (Under 18s). Based on that review, your child’s assessment will be carried out by Dr Sarah Castle, Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist.

    A child can be referred to Kinder Minds by their GP or another referrer, or by a parent or legal guardian directly. We accept referrals for children and young people under 18.

    All referrals are reviewed and triaged by our directors, including Dr Sarah Castle, Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist.

  2. 1 hour Online only Specialist Nurse

    Following completion of step 2 we will assist in booking the parental interview. This is a one-hour appointment with a set structure. The aim is to take a detailed history for your child’s ADHD assessment.

    This appointment is attended only by the parents or legal guardians — your child is not required to attend. This makes it easier for you to talk through your child’s strengths and challenges.

    It is led by one of our Specialist Nurses (Chris or Kezia), who will also be the one to deliver post-diagnostic support once the assessment is complete.

  3. Following completion of step 2 we will also send out the Conners-4 and Kinder Minds questionnaires needed to complete the ADHD diagnostic process. These are key for obtaining evidence of behaviours relevant to ADHD being observed across different settings.

    Family questionnaires

    • Conners-4 parent questionnaire Completed by you
    • Conners-4 self-report questionnaire Completed by your child if aged 9 or older

    School questionnaires

    • Conners-4 teacher questionnaire Completed by a teacher you select
    • Kinder Minds School Questionnaire Completed by the same teacher

    Choosing a teacher

    We recommend a teacher who has known your child for at least six months. If we are undertaking the assessment at the start of an academic year, or after a recent school change, then your child’s previous teacher is usually the best candidate for this part of the assessment.

  4. Your child’s case is discussed by our multidisciplinary team, bringing different expertise together before any conclusion is reached. Dr Sarah Castle leads this part, to ensure we have everything needed to complete the ADHD assessment.

  5. 1 hour In person or online Dr Sarah Castle

    This appointment is booked after completion of step 2, based on availability and allowing enough time for step 3 and step 4 to be completed.

    The one-hour feedback appointment is attended by your child and any parents or legal guardians, and can be held either online or in person at our Whangārei clinic. During the session, led by Dr Sarah Castle, Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist, the outcome of the assessment is discussed — and regardless of the diagnosis you will leave with a tailored management plan to fully support your child.

    Any appropriate medication options will also be mentioned, though no medication is started at this appointment. See If medication is recommended at the bottom of this page for how medication commencement works.

  6. Within two weeks of your feedback appointment we will email you a copy of the assessment report and invite your comments and any amendments. This review is your chance to correct anything before the report is finalised.

  7. Once the report is finalised we can send it to your GP on your behalf. As needed, we can also write a school letter to support your child’s education, complete a Child Disability Allowance form, and make onward referrals.

    Report to GP: optional School letter: as needed CDA form: as needed Onward referrals: as needed

  8. 1 hour In person or online Specialist Nurse

    To further support your child and family, we include with the assessment a one-hour post-diagnostic support appointment, which you can request to book directly with the Specialist Nurse who delivered the parental interview.

    This session is ideal for talking through the outcome of the diagnosis and what it means, asking questions, planning next steps, and getting practical advice on how to implement any of the advice you have received.

    Please book this within a month following the feedback appointment (step 6).

If medication is recommended

At Kinder Minds we do not start medication during the assessment. We consider this an important decision to be made together as a family, and we encourage you to first consider the recommended strategies and take your time deciding whether medication is right for you.

Both the feedback appointment (step 6) and the post-diagnostic support session (step 9) are ideal times to discuss any questions you may have about medication. If you choose to commence medication, or have already done so and need it reviewed, our ADHD Titration Clinic explains how a medication trial is carried out.
