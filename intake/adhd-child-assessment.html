Your steps

  1. Read about the assessment

    Our service leaflet explains the whole assessment — each stage, what it costs, and what you receive at the end.

    Open the service leaflet

  2. Complete your registration forms

    Which consent forms are needed depends on whether your child can give consent for themselves. Please answer the question below and we will show you exactly which forms to complete.

    Is your child 16 or older?

    Because your child is 16 or older, they can consent for themselves. Only one Combined Consent and Information Form is needed, completed by your child. A second guardian form is not required.

    • New Patient Registration Form Completed by you or your child Open form
    • Combined Consent and Information Form Completed by your child Open form

    Because your child is under 16, New Zealand law requires consent from all legal guardians for non-routine psychiatric services. Two copies of the Combined Consent and Information Form are needed — one for each parent or legal guardian.

    You can either forward this page to the other guardian, or let us know and we will contact them directly using the details you provide. Please tell us if more than two copies are needed.

    • New Patient Registration Form Completed once, by either guardian Open form
    • Combined Consent and Information Form — Guardian 1 Completed by the first parent or legal guardian Open form
    • Combined Consent and Information Form — Guardian 2 Completed by the second parent or legal guardian Open form

    We are not able to process any bookings until these forms are completed and returned.

  3. Complete the questionnaires

    Once your registration is complete we send these out at no additional cost. Because ADHD affects behaviour across different settings, we need information from more than one environment — usually home and school.

    • Conners-4 parent form — completed by you
    • Conners-4 teacher form — completed by a teacher who knows your child
    • Conners-4 self-report — completed by your child if they are 9 or older
    • Kinder Minds School Questionnaire — completed by the same teacher

    Choosing a teacher: pick someone who has known your child for at least six months and understands their behaviour at school.

  4. Book your structured interview

    A separate appointment — in clinic or by Zoom — to discuss your child and their strengths and difficulties. Our Clinical Nurse Specialist, Kezia Schroyen, will guide you through booking this on our Client Booking Portal once your forms are in.

  5. Attend the feedback & formulation appointment

    A one-hour appointment with either the Nurse Practitioner or a Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist, attended by the parent(s) or legal guardian(s) and your child. By the end of it we will discuss the outcome, develop a tailored management plan, and talk through medication options if appropriate.

  6. Review your draft report

    We email you a draft report within two weeks and invite your comments and any amendments. Once finalised it is sent to your GP, along with a letter to the school, a completed Child Disability Allowance form, and any onward referrals.

  7. Book your post-diagnostic support session

    A one-hour post-diagnostic support appointment is included in the cost of your assessment. Book it in the month following the diagnosis to talk through what it means, ask questions, and plan next steps together.

    Even if ADHD isn’t diagnosed this session is still highly valuable — it’s a chance to think about support options and practical strategies, whatever the outcome.

About medication

Medication is not started during the assessment itself. We encourage families to first consider the recommended strategies and take time to decide whether medication is right for your child.

If you later wish to explore it, get in touch and we will guide you through booking. Ongoing monitoring runs through our ADHD Titration Clinic, where we adjust and monitor the medication carefully with weekly support until your child is settled on the right dose.
