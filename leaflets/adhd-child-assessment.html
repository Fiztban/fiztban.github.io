ADHD Child Assessment Clinic

Information for parents and families

Welcome. This leaflet explains how our ADHD assessment for children works — the steps involved, what it costs, and what’s included. Our goal is to build a thorough, caring picture of your child from home, school and our own assessment, so that any diagnosis is accurate and you leave with a clear plan and support.

Appointments can be online, in person at our Whangārei clinic, or a mix of the two — whatever suits your family.

Your child’s assessment journey

Here’s each step, from your first referral through to a finalised report and support.

  1. Referral

    You can refer your child yourself, or your GP can refer them to Kinder Minds.

  2. Triage

    When your referral is triaged, we indicate its complexity and which clinician would be most appropriate. All cases are discussed in our multidisciplinary team meeting, with input from a Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist.

  3. Parental interview (ADHD Child Evaluation)

    A separate appointment — in clinic or by Zoom — for you to discuss your child and their strengths and difficulties. Our Clinical Nurse Specialist, Kezia Schroyen, books you in.

  4. Conners & additional questionnaires

    Conners questionnaires are sent to school, home and your child, along with additional questionnaires that screen for other challenges — so we hear from everyone around your child.

  5. Multidisciplinary team discussion

    Your child’s case is discussed by our multidisciplinary team, bringing different expertise together.

  6. Feedback & formulation appointment

    A one-hour appointment for feedback and formulation, with either the Nurse Practitioner or a Consultant Child & Adolescent Psychiatrist.

  7. Draft report & your input

    We email you a draft report within two weeks and invite your comments and any amendments.

  8. Finalised & shared

    The report is finalised and sent to your GP. We request a letter to the school, complete the Child Disability Allowance form, and make any onward referrals.

Support after the diagnosis

Included in the cost of your assessment is a one-hour post-diagnostic support appointment with our specialist nursing team or Occupational Therapist. You can book this in the month following your child’s diagnosis. It’s a chance to talk through what the diagnosis means, ask any questions, and plan the next steps together.

Even if ADHD isn’t diagnosed, this session is still highly valuable — it’s an opportunity to think about support options and practical strategies to help your child and family, whatever the outcome of the assessment.

If medication is recommended

If medication is recommended, this is discussed as part of your feedback and formulation session and may be prescribed then. Any further follow-up is $250 for a medication review with Dr Sarah Castle, and ongoing monitoring runs through our ADHD Titration Clinic — where we adjust and monitor the medication carefully with weekly support until your child is settled on the right dose.

Ask us for the separate ADHD Titration Clinic leaflet if you’d like to know how ongoing medication and monitoring works, and what it costs.

Additional service: a school visit or attendance at a school meeting by our nurse (within Northland) — to share additional strategies that may help at school — is available at $200 per hour.
