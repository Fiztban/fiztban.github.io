    It asks nothing about consent: that is the separate Consent and Information form. You will only be shown the parts that apply to you, and your answers are saved on this device as you go.

        Profile fields are not yet mapped

        Fourteen of this form’s inputs are Zanda ProfileField types, which write through to the client’s profile. Where their values sit inside the composition has not been observed yet, so the payload above writes them as plain text against their positions and that is almost certainly not what Zanda expects. Six of them — sex, gender identity, pronouns, region, how you heard about us, and insurer — also draw their choices from the practice settings rather than from the form, so this page offers free text with suggestions instead. Both are resolved the same way: enter a few values in the live form, save it, and read back where they landed.
