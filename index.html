What We Offer

Many of our services can be available via telehealth, please contact us to discuss your individual circumstances.

Comprehensive Autism Assessment

Comprehensive autism assessment for children and young people. It is built from the ADI-R developmental history interview with parents, the ADOS-2 observation with your child, and a multidisciplinary team review before your feedback and formulation appointment with Dr Castle. The ADOS-2 must be completed in person at our Whangārei clinic.

Assessment duration: minimum 3 appointments.

Cost:

  • $2,600 inc GST.
  • Adults: our focus is children and young people. Adult assessments are at Dr Castle's discretion. Please contact us to discuss.

Comprehensive ADHD Assessment

Comprehensive ADHD assessment for children and young people. It is built from a structured parental interview, the Conners-4 questionnaires completed at home and at school, and a multidisciplinary team review before your feedback appointment.

Assessment duration: minimum 2 appointments.

Pathways and cost:

  • Standard pathway, led by our Nurse Practitioner: $1,500 inc GST.
  • Complex pathway, carried out by Dr Castle: $1,800 inc GST.
  • Adults: our focus is children and young people. Adult assessments are at Dr Castle's discretion. Please contact us to discuss.

Combined ADHD & Autism Assessment

Where both ADHD and autism are being considered, we can assess for both in a single pathway.

It includes the parental interview, the Conners-4 questionnaires, the ADI-R developmental history interview, the ADOS-2 observation, a multidisciplinary team review, and a feedback and formulation appointment with Dr Castle.

Assessment duration: minimum 4 appointments.

Cost: $3,000 inc GST

ADHD Titration Clinic New service model

Our new model for starting and managing ADHD medication, replacing per-appointment and per-prescription billing with a single monthly fee.

Starting and adjusting ADHD medication with close weekly support, until the dose is settled and your GP can take over prescribing.

You begin with one appointment to confirm it is safe to start. Ongoing care then runs as a monthly fee covering weekly nurse follow-up, dose guidance and your prescriptions, sent electronically to your pharmacy.

Open to people we have diagnosed and people diagnosed elsewhere.

Cost:

  • $250 inc GST first appointment, if we diagnosed the ADHD.
  • $350 inc GST first appointment, if it was diagnosed elsewhere.
  • $150 inc GST per calendar month, stimulant monitoring.
  • $50 inc GST per calendar month, atomoxetine-only monitoring.

Post-Diagnostic Support

Available to clients diagnosed with us, and to those diagnosed elsewhere.

Every neurodevelopmental assessment at Kinder Minds includes one hour of post-diagnostic support, to help families navigate their outcome.

From there you can carry on for as long as you find it useful. That might be understanding the diagnosis, what living with it looks like day to day, or how to put effective strategies in place to support your lives.

We start with a free 15-minute phone call to agree the approach, the focus and the schedule you want.

Session duration: as agreed, minimum 30 minutes.

Cost: $150 inc GST per hour

Prescriptions

All prescriptions are included in the monthly subscription of our ADHD Titration Clinic, sent electronically to your pharmacy at no extra cost.

Outside the subscription we can issue new prescriptions at the doctor's discretion, charged as a flat fee per script.

Cost: $35 inc GST

Our Approach

At Kinder Minds, our approach to mental health care for young people is deeply rooted in compassion, expertise, and a commitment to transformative healing. Under the leadership of Sarah, our medical director, we offer a sanctuary for children and adolescents grappling with a range of mental health challenges. We strive to make every young person feel understood, respected, and supported, as we navigate the intricacies of their mental health with them.

Our clinic's philosophy is centered on a compassionate understanding of the unique experiences and challenges faced by young people, and our mission is to transform their lives by delving into their inner world, empowering them with the tools they need to revover and build lasting resilience.

Thanks to Sarah's extensive training in Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, with specialised knowledge in trauma and neurodiversity (including Autism and ADHD), we are able to offer holistic and innovative interventions capable of addressing a wide range of mental health challenges to positively impact the lives of young people.

Sarah Castle

Consultant Child and Adolescent Psychiatrist

Dr. Sarah Castle is at the heart of Kinder Minds' approach and commitment to helping young people and their families overcome the mental health challenges they face. Sarah brings a wealth of psychiatric and therapeutic expertise gained through her extensive training. Sarah held the position of Clinical Head of Department for Child and Adolescent Mental Health, Early intervention in Psychosis, Maternal Infant Mental Health and Forensic CAMHS Services, in Te Tai Tokerau DHB across Northland.

Alongside formal clinical training, Sarah is passionate about working alongside families and Young People, to understand their inner world, concerns, worries, fantasies and dreams matter by giving them a voice. She often tries to work creativity and works outside of a one size fits all approach, to connect with ‘hard to reach’ children and young people.

Sarah has a keen interest in the assessment and treatment of trauma and neurodiversity, including ASD and ADHD. Her expertise is broadened by additional training in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), Mentalization Based Therapy for Adolescents, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for trauma, Dialectical Behavioural Therapy, and other talking therapies. Sarah is particularly skilled in supporting young people through the diagnostic process, including ADOS-II, and offering interventions that benefit them and their family’s post-diagnosis, demonstrating her comprehensive approach to mental health care.

Kezia Schroyen

Clinical Nurse Specialist

Kezia (she/her) is a Registered Nurse that works closely with Dr Castle and Kinder Minds helping with assessments, medication follow ups, and post-diagnostic support for families and young people. Having returned to her Whangārei origins in 2019 with her young family Kezia is also a mother of two primary school aged children and enjoys baking, crafting, and gaming.

Kezia brings a wealth of both experience and training having completed her specialisation in Neurodevelopmental Paediatrics at the Child Health Centre in Whangārei over the past 5 years. With a Post-Graduate Diploma in Child Health and her lived experience of neurodivergence, Kezia continues to further her training following her passion to help children and young adults be supported and better understood for who they are. Kezia is able to administer the following standardised assessments:

  • ADOS-II - Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule
  • ADI-R - Autism Diagnostic Interview
  • Griffiths-III - Griffiths Scales of Child Development
  • KBIT-2 - Kaufman Brief Intelligence Test
