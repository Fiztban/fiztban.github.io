Assessment components and pathway options

An ADHD assessment cannot rest on a single appointment. Diagnostic criteria ask for evidence of how your child is across more than one setting, so the assessment is built from two components: a structured interview with you, and questionnaires completed at home and at school.

ADHD Child Evaluation (ACE) or Young DIVA

1 hour Online only Nurse Specialist

A structured interview about your child’s development and the difficulties you see day to day. Your child does not attend, so you can speak freely.

If either assessment component is not completed, we cannot finish the assessment.

Both components are brought to our multidisciplinary team meeting, led by Dr Castle, before the final appointment. Note that neither component on its own produces a diagnosis.

When the component prices apply

The component prices above are charged only when the assessment does not run to completion — either because you choose to stop after a component is finished, or because what we have learned points somewhere else.

During the interview, or as the questionnaires come back, the picture can change. If it does, we would discuss the options with you. Typically these are:

  • ADHD does not seem likely, and we suggest discontinuing the assessment.
  • Autism seems more likely than ADHD, and we suggest moving to our Autism (ASD) Assessment.
  • Both ADHD and autism seem likely, and we suggest moving to our Combined ADHD & Autism Assessment.

Where we move to another assessment, any component already completed that also forms part of that pathway is covered by its price — you are not charged twice. Only components that sit outside the new pathway are charged at the prices above, and we will always set out the figures with you first.

ADHD medication

ADHD medication is often a helpful part of managing ADHD, and we can offer to start and manage it following a positive diagnosis. Medication commencement and management is a separate service, started after completion of this assessment. Read below for more information.

  3. 1 hour Online only Nurse Specialist

    Following completion of step 2 we will assist in booking the parental interview. This is a one-hour appointment with a set structure. The aim is to take a detailed history for your child’s ADHD assessment.

    This appointment is attended only by the parents or legal guardians — your child is not required to attend. This makes it easier for you to talk through your child’s strengths and challenges.

    It is led by one of our Nurse Specialists (Chris or Kezia), who will also be the one to deliver post-diagnostic support once the assessment is complete.

  4. Following completion of step 2 we will also send out the Conners-4 and Kinder Minds questionnaires needed to complete the ADHD diagnostic process. These are key for obtaining evidence of behaviours relevant to ADHD being observed across different settings.

    Family questionnaires

    • Conners-4 parent questionnaire Completed by you
    • Conners-4 self-report questionnaire Completed by your child if aged 9 or older

    School questionnaires

    • Conners-4 teacher questionnaire Completed by a teacher you select
    • Kinder Minds School Questionnaire Completed by the same teacher

    Choosing a teacher

    We recommend a teacher who has known your child for at least six months. If we are undertaking the assessment at the start of an academic year, or after a recent school change, then your child’s previous teacher is usually the best candidate for this part of the assessment.

  5. Your child’s case is discussed by our multidisciplinary team, bringing different expertise together before any conclusion is reached. Dr Castle leads this part, to ensure we have everything needed to complete the ADHD assessment.

  6. 1 hour In person or online Nurse Practitioner Dr Castle

    This appointment is booked after completion of step 2, based on availability and allowing enough time for step 3 and step 4 to be completed.

    The one-hour diagnosis appointment is attended by your child and any parents or legal guardians, and can be held either online or in person at our Whangārei clinic. During the session, led by our Nurse PractitionerDr Castle, the outcome of the assessment is discussed — and regardless of the diagnosis you will leave with a tailored management plan to fully support your child.

    Any appropriate medication options will also be mentioned, though no medication is started at this appointment. See If medication is recommended at the bottom of this page for how medication commencement works.

  7. Within two weeks of your diagnosis appointment we will email you a copy of the assessment report and invite your comments and any amendments. This review is your chance to correct anything before the report is finalised.

  8. Once the report is finalised we can send it to your GP on your behalf. As needed, we can also write a school letter to support your child’s education, complete a Child Disability Allowance form, and make onward referrals.

    Report to GP: optional School letter: as needed CDA form: as needed Onward referrals: as needed

  9. 1 hour In person or online Nurse Specialist

    To further support your child and family, we include with the assessment a one-hour post-diagnostic support appointment, which you can request to book directly with the Nurse Specialist who delivered the parental interview.

    This session is ideal for talking through the outcome of the diagnosis and what it means, asking questions, planning next steps, and getting practical advice on how to implement any of the advice you have received.

    Please book this within a month following the diagnosis appointment (step 6).

If medication is recommended

At Kinder Minds we do not start medication during the assessment. We consider this an important decision to be made together as a family, and we encourage you to first consider the recommended strategies and take your time deciding whether medication is right for you.

Both the diagnosis appointment (step 6) and the post-diagnostic support session (step 9) are ideal times to discuss any questions you may have about medication. If you choose to commence medication, or have already done so and need it reviewed, our ADHD Titration Clinic explains how a medication trial is carried out.

Support after the diagnosis

Your pathway includes a one-hour post-diagnostic support session with one of our Nurse Specialists, which you can book in the month following your child’s assessment (step 9). It is a chance to talk through what the diagnosis means, ask questions, and plan the next steps together.

If further therapeutic support would be helpful after that, we can refer you on to our post-diagnostic clinic.

School visit or school meeting

A nurse from our team can attend a school visit or school meeting (within Northland), to share additional strategies that may help support your child’s education. This is charged at $200 per hour (inc GST).
